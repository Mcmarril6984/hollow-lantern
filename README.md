# Hollow Lantern

A fast 2D action roguelite in a lantern-lit underworld. Each run grows new levels, each death sends you back to the Quiet Hearth with your embers, and each route through the world leads somewhere different.

## Features
- **Ten biomes and three bosses.** Flooded cisterns, a burnt orchard, bell towers, crystal steppes, buried tombs, root caverns, ruined ramparts, lava forges, moonlit spires and the machine at the heart of the world. Every run passes two biomes, a boss, two biomes, a boss, two biomes and the final boss, choosing a branch at each exit.
- **Levels that grow every run.** Corridors, shafts, towers and halls branch into side wings with treasure, shrines and merchants. The map remembers what you've explored, and teleport stones link what you've found.
- **Over forty enemies, each with an attack of its own.** Sludge that slows you, crabs that snap twice and skitter off, bells that ring shockwaves, archers who rain arrows from above, burrowers that erupt beneath you, mortars that shell marked craters, drones that ricochet around the room, and many more.
- **Bosses built around their own ideas.** The Brine Matron floods her arena and drives you onto the ledges. The Gearwright Seraph sweeps the room with a beam you must roll through. The Hollow Sovereign puts out the lights and sends shadows of you after you.
- **Make your hero.** Pick body, face, hair, armour pieces and colours.
- **Play your way.** Keyboard, controller (Switch Pro, Xbox and PlayStation controllers through macOS) or touch.

## Controls
Move: A/D or stick · Jump: Space/A · Roll: Shift/B · Attack: J/X · Skill: K/Y · Switch weapon: Q/L · Flask: F/R · Interact: E/ZR · Map: M, Tab or − · Hero editor: C · Pause: Esc or +

## Requirements
macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. Unzip, then right-click Hollow Lantern.app and choose Open the first time (the app is not notarized).
